# Reviewing Chup!'s UI from screenshots

Render the screens (`--render-design`, plus `CHUP_SCROLL_REVIEW=1` and `CHUP_NOTES_PREVIEW=1`). Then check them at the same window size and display scale. Checklist from a Codex design review, 2026-11-06:

- **Hierarchy:** can you name the page, its content and its primary action within two seconds?
- **Density:** is useful content pushed aside by padding, oversized controls or illustration?
- **Alignment:** do titles, sections, fields and footers share visible leading edges?
- **Rhythm:** does spacing follow 4/8/12/16/24/32 pt, with larger gaps for larger breaks?
- **Contrast:** can you read metadata and find controls without leaning in?
- **Surfaces:** does each card, border or shadow express a real grouping or elevation?
- **State:** are selection, recording, disabled actions and toggle positions unambiguous?
- **Consistency:** placed side by side, do type, icons, radii and buttons look related?

Its main diagnosis was that everything has the same pale tone, generous padding, rounded shape and medium weight. The result is soft but has little structure or authority. It named the populated summary (render 02b) as the strongest screen and the model for the others.
